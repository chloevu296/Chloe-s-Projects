# Business Forecasting using Regression Analysis

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the Business Forecasting unit at Macquarie University. Acting as a Business Analytics Consultant for Australian Building Approvals, I developed and evaluated regression-based forecasting models to predict monthly dwelling approvals and provide evidence-based business recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Accurate forecasts of dwelling approvals are essential for planning resources, investment and operations within the construction industry. This project investigated historical ABS building approval data to develop forecasting models, evaluate their performance and recommend the most appropriate model for business decision-making.

---

## My Contributions

* Developed regression-based forecasting models to predict monthly dwelling approvals using historical ABS data.
* Evaluated model performance using statistical measures and diagnostic testing to assess forecasting accuracy and reliability.
* Compared regression models with alternative forecasting approaches and critically justified the preferred model from both forecasting and business intelligence perspectives.
* Interpreted forecasting results to generate actionable business insights and strategic recommendations.
* Communicated findings through an executive-style presentation supported by clear visualisations and data analysis.
